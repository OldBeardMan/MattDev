About me

Computer Science Engineer • Python Developer • IT Specialist

Mateusz Krupa - Computer Science engineer, Python developer and IT specialist. I have been programming since primary school, when I created my first JavaScript games and published them on my own website built with HTML and CSS. In the years that followed, I delivered increasingly complex application and web projects.

In 2026, I earned my Bachelor of Engineering degree from Lublin University of Technology. During my studies, I developed Python applications and completed commercial website development projects.

Professionally, I combine two areas of expertise. The first is software development - simulation and optimization algorithms, energy models, process automation and applications that support engineering teams. The second is IT infrastructure administration - the Microsoft 365 platform, Linux servers, computer networks and organization-wide technical support.

I have gained my experience in the energy sector, working on battery energy storage projects and renewable ammonia production plants. In parallel, I run my own business, Usługi Informatyczne „MatIf”, through which I deliver web projects and IT support for companies.
