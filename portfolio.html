A company website for Tomasz Krupa Doradztwo Techniczne (TKDT) - a consulting firm serving the chemical and energy industries as well as the renewable energy and hydrogen sectors. A restrained, typography-driven one-page layout guides the reader from the scope of services and the company's approach to a table of completed projects and a request-for-quote form.

Vibe Tuner

  • Emotion detection from a face photo Music matched to emotions
  • Emotion history Emotion analytics over time
  • Python Engineering thesis - Lublin University of Technology

Vibe Tuner is an application I developed as my engineering thesis at Lublin University of Technology. Based on a photo of the user's face, it recognizes their emotions and automatically selects matching music. The application stores a history of detected emotions and provides an analytics module showing how the user's mood has changed over time.
